[C] `sample_sqrt_2e3c88` in [internal_analysis.hpp](/home/john/Desktop/cheat_re/source/reconstructed/internal_analysis.hpp) translates `0x2e3c88–0x2e3ce1`. It reinterprets the input float as 32 bits, arithmetic-shifts that signed word right once, subtracts it from `0x5f3759df`, performs two inverse-root Newton iterations and returns the reciprocal. [I] Unsigned bit operations express the signed shift/subtraction without host signed-overflow behavior.
