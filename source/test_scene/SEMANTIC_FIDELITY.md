## Evidence discipline

Current results come only from regenerated strict, sanitizer, Wine, CMake, full
reconstruction and aggregate records described in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).
The audit binds source/dependency/compiler/artifact hashes, runtime/event/entrypoint
bytes and capture hashes. Every positive capture must independently report complete
status and recorded submission. Trace counters describe explicit entries, caller
attribution or typed boundaries; they are not original instruction coverage.

135 original inputs remain hash-checked, including the virtual-layout DLL with
SHA256 `ad5ad10d4fbb818064a4d1ce245c6c586394026c2296c94d2eb99e7487ee2090`.
Native Windows and inside Rust remain unvalidated. Matching GameAssembly.dll,
UnityPlayer.dll and global-metadata.dat remain unavailable. They are required for
build-specific ABI/engine validation, while the local implementation backlog stays
visible in REQUIREMENTS.md. No original sample or live foreign address is executed.
