### P2 — E2: Entity-to-Transform routing and its rejection gate are skipped

[C] The original [player loop](/home/john/Desktop/cheat_re/dump_analysis/semantic_reconstruction/world_esp/3098a4.txt:4515) calls **`0x315a54` at 0x30ec1e**, validates the returned pointer with `0x2caf08` at **0x30ec29**, rejects invalid results at **0x30ec30**, and passes that returned Transform to **`0x3747d4` at 0x30ec40**. The recovered [315a54 wrapper](/home/john/Desktop/cheat_re/dump_analysis/semantic_reconstruction/game_runtime_wrappers/315a54.asm) resolves `UnityEngine.Component.get_transform`, preserving its receiver guard, cache/context setup, and final object result.

[C] [world_esp_player_worker.cpp](/home/john/Desktop/cheat_re/source/reconstructed/world_esp_player_worker.cpp:96) instead calls `b.game.position_3747d4(e)` directly. [esp.cpp](/home/john/Desktop/cheat_re/source/test_scene/esp.cpp:226) supplies `transforms.at(entity)`, and stores the entity's position under that entity key. There is no independent component-to-Transform key/result for the quad center. The bone path does use distinct Transform pointers; this finding concerns the separate entity-position path.

[I] Supplying the final Unity position is a reasonable owned boundary, but this boundary currently absorbs **both** the component lookup and position getter and omits the intervening native rejection. A live binder that simply forwards this callback as `Transform.get_position(entity)` would use the wrong receiver type. Even offline, a valid entity with a missing component Transform cannot exercise the original skip branch. This should be recorded as a controller composition gap rather than “only final transform positions are supplied.”

[I] Targeted test: give entity, component Transform, bone-7 Transform, and bone-53 Transform four distinct keys with distinguishable positions. Require `315a54(entity)` before `3747d4(component_transform)`. Return a null/out-of-range Transform while leaving entity validity and bones valid; require no quad projection/box/name for that entity and continuation to the next record. Stop on an unavailable required lookup rather than inventing a Transform.
